Industries We Serve

Specialized experience across the most demanding home service sectors. Our secretaries know your industry.

Which industries does Top Dispatch serve?

Top Dispatch staffs remote receptionists and dispatchers for US home service trades: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, locksmith, roofing, garage door, pest control, cleaning, landscaping, construction and general contracting, security and alarm, chimney, air duct cleaning and medical spas. Each secretary is trained on the vocabulary and call types of the trade they support.

HVAC

Appointment setting, emergency dispatch, seasonal scheduling and technician routing for heating and cooling companies.

  • No-heat and no-cool emergency calls
  • Seasonal tune-up and maintenance booking
  • Maintenance-plan renewals
  • Thermostat and filter troubleshooting questions
HVAC answering service

Plumbing

Emergency call triaging, booking, dispatch management and CRM updates for plumbing companies of all sizes.

  • Drain cleaning and camera inspections
Plumbing answering service
