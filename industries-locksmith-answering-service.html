Why Locksmith Calls Need a Specialist

Locksmith work is almost entirely about speed. A caller locked out of their car or home is ringing several companies at once and will book whoever answers and gives a clear price and arrival time. An unanswered call is nearly always a lost job.

Locksmith intake also needs care. Confirming location, vehicle details and verification requirements up front avoids wasted trips and protects you from being used for unauthorised entry.

A dedicated receptionist answers instantly, quotes from your price list including after-hours and mileage charges, collects what the tech needs and dispatches the nearest available locksmith.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a locksmith intake script you approve. These are the questions most locksmith owners start with:

  1. Is this a home, vehicle, business or safe?
  2. What's the exact location, including cross streets or parking spot?
  3. For vehicles: make, model and year, and are keys lost or locked inside?
  4. Is anyone, a child or pet, locked inside?
  5. Can the caller show ID or proof of residence on arrival?
  6. Is this a lockout, rekey, lock change or new key?
  7. Is this for a business or property management account?
  8. Best callback number while waiting?

Demand patterns

Lockouts spike on weekends, evenings and holidays, and car lockouts rise in extreme heat and cold. Coverage is built around those hours rather than a standard 9-to-5.

Locksmith Answering Service FAQ

What locksmith owners ask before they sign up.

Do you provide 24/7 locksmith dispatch?

Yes, with after-hours coverage your receptionist dispatches around the clock using your rota.

Can they quote prices?

They quote from your approved price list, including after-hours and mileage charges.
