Why Landscaping Calls Need a Specialist

Landscaping runs on seasonal contracts and weather. In spring, phones fill with estimate requests at the same time crews are busiest. In rainy weeks, whole routes need moving and every customer needs a call or text.

Most landscaping owners spend evenings returning calls and rearranging schedules. That's time not spent selling larger design and install work.

A dedicated receptionist books estimates, signs up seasonal maintenance, reschedules for weather and follows up on open quotes, so crews keep moving and the pipeline keeps filling.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a landscaping intake script you approve. These are the questions most landscaping owners start with:

  1. Is this weekly maintenance, a one-time cleanup or a design and install project?
  2. Property address and approximate lot size?
  3. Residential, HOA or commercial?
  4. Any irrigation, lighting or hardscape needs?
  5. Preferred service day and frequency?
  6. Any gate codes, pets or access notes?
  7. Budget range for project work?
  8. How did you hear about us?
