Our Services

Comprehensive virtual staffing solutions designed specifically for US home service businesses.

What services does Top Dispatch offer?

Top Dispatch provides eight remote staffing services for US home service companies: virtual receptionist, dispatch coordination, after-hours answering, appointment setting, cold calling and lead generation, back-office management, reporting and analytics, and customer support. Each is delivered by one dedicated, trained secretary who works your hours inside your existing software.

Virtual Receptionist

Every call answered in your company's name by someone who knows your services, service area and prices.

  • Inbound calls answered in your business name
  • New-customer intake and address verification
  • Quotes from your price sheet and diagnostic fees
  • Job booking into ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber and similar
  • Messages and call summaries for the owner
  • Warm transfers to you or a technician
Explore virtual receptionist

Dispatch Coordination

Jobs go to the right technician, the tech gets the details and the customer hears when to expect them.

  • Real-time technician assignment and routing
  • Schedule board management in your software
  • Customer arrival-time (ETA) texts and calls
  • Emergency and after-hours dispatch
  • Job status tracking and close-out checks
  • Reshuffling around cancellations and overruns
Explore dispatch coordination

After-Hours Answering

Evening, weekend and overnight calls answered by a person who follows your emergency rules.

  • Evening, overnight and weekend shifts
  • Urgent versus can-wait triage on your rules
  • On-call technician paging
  • Emergency fee quoting
  • Next-morning job booking
  • Holiday coverage
Explore after-hours answering

Appointment Setting

Bookings made, confirmed and reminded, and unsold estimates chased while they're still warm.

  • Inbound call answering and booking
  • Calendar and scheduling management
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Cancellation and reschedule handling
  • Outbound follow-up on open estimates
  • Maintenance-plan visit booking
Explore appointment setting

Cold Calling & Lead Gen

Outbound calls to prospects, past customers and lapsed maintenance plans that end with a visit booked.

  • Past-customer reactivation calls
  • Lapsed maintenance-plan renewals
  • Commercial and property-manager prospecting
  • Lead qualification and scoring
  • Booked visits for sales reps
  • Pipeline and CRM updates
Explore cold calling & lead gen

Back Office Management

Invoicing, data entry, email, CRM upkeep and document prep handled while you're in the field.

  • Invoicing and billing support
  • Payment reminder calls and emails
  • Data entry and job record clean-up
  • Shared inbox management
  • CRM and customer database upkeep
  • Estimate and proposal preparation

Reporting & Analytics

Regular performance reports, job summaries and data so you always know how operations are running.

  • Weekly and monthly operations reports
  • Call volume and answer-rate tracking
  • Booking rate by source
  • Job performance summaries
  • Open estimate pipeline value
  • Revenue tracking support

Customer Support

Questions, complaints and after-job check-ins handled before they turn into bad reviews.

  • Phone and email customer support
  • Complaint handling and escalation
  • After-job satisfaction calls
  • Review requests to happy customers
