What is a 24/7 answering service?

A 24/7 answering service answers your business phone live at any hour, so callers reach a person instead of voicemail. Top Dispatch provides a dedicated, trained receptionist who answers in your company name, books appointments into your software, follows your emergency rules after hours and sends you a summary of every call.

Why live answering beats voicemail

Most callers who reach voicemail don't leave a message. They hang up and call the next business on the list. For service businesses, that next business often gets the job, the review and the repeat customer.

Live answering keeps those callers. A trained person confirms you can help, gives them a next step and books the work, which is what the caller wanted in the first place.

Message-taking vs. a receptionist who books

Traditional answering services take a message and pass it on. You still have to call back, by which time the caller may have booked elsewhere.

A Top Dispatch receptionist goes further. They check your service area, quote what you allow, book the slot in your calendar and tell the customer what happens next. Messages are only for the calls that genuinely need you.

Business hours, after hours and overflow

Business hours: your receptionist is your front desk, answering every call and handling bookings and questions.

After hours: nights and weekends are covered with triage based on your rules. Emergencies reach your on-call tech, and everything else is booked for the morning.

Overflow: when your office line is busy, calls roll to your receptionist instead of voicemail.

You can choose any combination. See after-hours answering for how night calls are handled.

What to look for in an answering service

  • A live person, not an automated menu
  • Your company name in the greeting
  • Ability to book appointments, not just take messages
  • Clear emergency rules and on-call paging
  • Flat, predictable pricing
  • No long-term contract

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the answering service really 24/7?

Coverage is built around the hours you choose, up to 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.

Will callers hear my business name?

Yes. Your receptionist answers with your exact greeting.

Can the answering service book appointments?

Yes. Your receptionist books directly into your calendar or field-service software.

How are emergencies handled at night?

Your receptionist follows the emergency rules you set and pages your on-call technician only for genuine emergencies.

Do you charge per minute?

No. Top Dispatch charges a flat monthly fee based on the hours covered.

Can I use the service only for overflow?

Yes. Calls your office can't answer roll over to your receptionist.

How do I get messages?

Through a call summary by email or text, and notes in your CRM.

How quickly can it start?

Most clients are live within 48 to 72 hours.
