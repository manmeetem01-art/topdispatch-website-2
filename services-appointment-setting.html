What is an appointment setting service for contractors?

An appointment setting service handles the calls that fill and protect your schedule. A Top Dispatch appointment setter books inbound requests, confirms upcoming jobs, sends reminders to reduce no-shows, reschedules cancellations and calls back open estimates, with every booking entered in your calendar or CRM.

Why a full calendar depends on follow-up, not just answering

Most home service businesses already generate more interest than they convert. Estimates go out and are never chased. Maintenance customers forget their annual visit. Tomorrow's jobs aren't confirmed, so techs arrive at empty houses.

Each of those gaps is small on its own. Together they leave revenue sitting in your CRM. An appointment setter's job is to close them every day: book what comes in, confirm what's scheduled, and call back what's still open.

Because the same person handles your inbound calls and your follow-ups, they know which customers already spoke to you, what was quoted and what the hesitation was. That context makes follow-up calls feel like service, not sales.

Appointment Setting FAQ

Straight answers to the questions owners ask most.

Do you only book inbound calls?

No. Appointment setting covers inbound bookings plus outbound work such as confirmations, reminders and estimate follow-up.

Will reminders reduce my no-shows?

Confirming each job the day before gives customers a chance to reschedule instead of not being home, and frees the slot to rebook.

Can the setter book into my software?

Yes. They book directly in your field-service software or calendar using a user login.

How is this different from cold calling?

Appointment setting works with people who already contacted you or are your customers. Cold calling reaches new prospects. See our cold calling service for outbound lead generation.

Can you book from web form leads?

Yes. Leads from your website or ads can be called and booked within minutes during covered hours.

Do you send text reminders?

Your setter sends reminders through your software or phone system using templates you approve.

Can you handle multiple calendars?

Yes, including separate calendars for techs, estimators and crews.

How fast do you call web leads?

During covered hours, new leads are called as soon as they arrive.
