Why support after the job drives reviews and repeat work

For home service businesses, online reviews are often the deciding factor between you and the next listing. Most unhappy customers don't complain to you first. They post. Most happy ones never think to leave a review unless asked.

Customer support after the job addresses both. A quick check-in call catches problems while they can still be fixed. A timely review request turns satisfied customers into public proof.

Your Top Dispatch support specialist handles questions, complaints and follow-ups by phone and email, using the tone and limits you set, and logs everything so your team learns from it.

Support habits that hurt reputation

Slow replies

A complaint left overnight often becomes a one-star review.

Defensive tone

Customers want to be heard before they hear explanations.

Asking selectively

Platforms discourage review gating. Follow their rules when requesting reviews.

No record

Patterns in complaints are only visible when every contact is logged.

Customer Support FAQ

Straight answers to the questions owners ask most.

Can you respond to online reviews?

Your specialist can draft responses for your approval or post them under your guidelines.

Do you handle angry customers?

Yes. Specialists are trained to listen, de-escalate and resolve within the limits you set, and to escalate anything beyond them.

Is review gating allowed?

Platforms such as Google discourage asking only happy customers for reviews. We follow your policy and platform rules when sending requests.

Is support by phone only?

Phone and email are standard. Text and chat can be added if your tools support it.

Can you reply to Google reviews?

We draft replies for your approval, or post under your guidelines if you prefer.

Do you handle warranty claims?

Yes. We log the claim, check terms and schedule a revisit within the rules you set.

Can support work in Spanish?

Tell us if you need bilingual support and we'll check availability for your match.

Is customer support separate from reception?

It can be combined in the same role, which is common for smaller businesses.
