Why Contractor Calls Need a Specialist

General contractors and remodelers juggle long projects, many stakeholders and a steady flow of new enquiries. When the owner is on a job site, new leads wait, subcontractors can't confirm, and clients feel ignored.

Project work also needs qualifying. Not every caller with a remodel idea has the budget or timeline that fits your business, and site visits are expensive to waste.

A dedicated receptionist and coordinator qualifies leads, books site visits, schedules subs and inspections, and keeps clients informed, so projects stay on track and good leads don't go cold.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a contractor intake script you approve. These are the questions most contractor owners start with:

  1. What type of project: kitchen, bath, addition, full remodel or repair?
  2. Property type and approximate age?
  3. Budget range?
  4. Desired start date and any deadlines?
  5. Do you have plans, drawings or an HOA approval?
  6. Are you the owner and decision-maker?
  7. Any permits already in place?
  8. How did you hear about us?
