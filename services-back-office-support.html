What back-office tasks can a remote assistant handle for a contractor?

A remote back-office assistant handles the admin that slows a trade business down: sending and chasing invoices, entering job and customer data, keeping the CRM clean, answering the shared inbox, preparing estimates and documents, and tracking permits or warranties. At Top Dispatch this is often the same person who answers your phones, so they already know every job.

Back Office Management FAQ

Straight answers to the questions owners ask most.

Will a remote assistant have access to my bank account?

No. Your assistant needs access only to the tools the work requires, such as your job software, inbox and invoicing tool. Payments and banking stay with you.

Can the same person answer calls and do admin?

Yes. Many clients fill quieter phone hours with back-office work, which makes one full-time receptionist good value.

Which tools can they use?

QuickBooks, your field-service software, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and most common CRMs.

Is my data secure?

Access is granted per tool with your own logins and can be removed at any time. We recommend role-based permissions so your assistant sees only what they need.
