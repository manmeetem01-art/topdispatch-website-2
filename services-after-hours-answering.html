How does an after-hours answering service work for trades?

An after-hours answering service picks up your calls outside business hours. With Top Dispatch a trained receptionist works your evening, overnight or weekend shift, uses your emergency rules to decide what is urgent, pages your on-call technician for genuine emergencies and books routine requests into the next available slot, so you are only woken for work that pays.

Why after-hours calls are your most valuable calls

Burst pipes, no-heat calls, lockouts and broken garage springs don't wait for business hours. They're also the calls where customers are least price-sensitive and most likely to book the first company that answers. Sending them to voicemail hands that work to a competitor.

The opposite problem is just as costly: an owner who answers every after-hours call personally is woken at 2 AM for a quote request that could have waited until morning. Over a season that wears people down and leads to missed emergencies anyway.

A trained after-hours receptionist sits between the two. They answer every call live, decide whether it's a real emergency using your rules, page your on-call tech only when it is, and book everything else into the first slot of the next day.

How after-hours triage works

Your receptionist follows a short decision path on every night or weekend call:

What we need from you

  • Your written emergency rules and examples
  • After-hours, weekend and holiday rates
  • On-call rota with phone numbers and backup contacts
  • Safety scripts for gas, electrical and flooding calls
  • Which calls should never be paged overnight
  • Hours you want covered: evenings, overnight, weekends, holidays
Step 1

Answer live

Your company name, a calm voice and a quick question about what's happening.

Step 2

Check for safety

Gas smell, sparks, smoke or flooding near electrics trigger your safety script first, including telling the caller to call 911 where appropriate.

Step 3

Apply your emergency rules

For example: no heat below 55°F, active leak, no power to the whole home, locked out with a child inside.

Step 4

Quote the after-hours fee

Callers hear the emergency rate before a tech is sent, so there are no disputes later.

Step 5

Page the on-call tech

With the address, problem and customer contact details. If they don't respond, your escalation chain is followed.

Step 6

Book the rest for morning

Non-urgent requests get the first available slot and a confirmation.

Step 7

Morning handover

You start the day with a list of everything that came in overnight and what was done.

What goes wrong without proper after-hours coverage

Voicemail at night

Emergency callers rarely leave messages. They call the next listing.

Owner burnout

Answering every night call personally isn't sustainable through a peak season.

No fee disclosure

Sending a tech without quoting the after-hours rate leads to arguments at the door.

No escalation chain

If the on-call tech is asleep or out of signal, someone else needs to be reached.

After-Hours Answering FAQ

Straight answers to the questions owners ask most.

Is after-hours coverage a person or a machine?

A person. Your after-hours receptionist is a trained Top Dispatch team member working a scheduled shift, not voicemail or an automated menu.

Can I get only weekend coverage?

Yes. Coverage is built around the hours you need, whether that is weeknights, weekends, holidays or full overnight.

What happens if my on-call tech doesn't answer?

Your receptionist follows your escalation chain, for example a second tech and then the owner, and keeps the customer updated.

Do you charge per call after hours?

No. Top Dispatch charges a flat monthly fee based on the hours covered, not per call or per minute.

Can you cover just holidays?

Yes. Holiday-only or peak-season coverage can be arranged.

Will my customers know it's after-hours coverage?

They hear your company name and a trained person. Most can't tell it isn't your office.

Do you charge more for overnight shifts?

Pricing depends on the hours covered. Tell us your schedule and we quote a flat monthly fee.

Can after-hours calls be booked into my software?

Yes. Your receptionist books routine work directly into your calendar or field-service software.
