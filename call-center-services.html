Why small businesses outsource their call center

Large companies run call centers because volume justifies it. Small businesses face the same problem at a smaller scale: calls come in while everyone is busy, follow-ups slip and the owner ends up answering the phone at night. Hiring a full-time local employee for the phones is expensive, slow and hard to cover for sickness and holidays.

An outsourced call center solves the coverage problem without the overhead. Traditional providers do this with a pool of agents answering many companies' lines from one script. That works for simple message-taking, but it breaks down when callers need real answers, quotes or a booked appointment.

Top Dispatch takes a different approach. You get one dedicated agent who learns your business, so the service behaves like your own call center team, just located remotely.

Inbound and outbound in one role

Inbound: new-customer calls, existing customers, quotes, bookings, rescheduling, billing questions, after-hours emergencies and overflow when your office line is busy.

Outbound: estimate follow-up, appointment confirmations, maintenance reminders, reactivating past customers, renewing service plans and commercial prospecting.

Because the same person handles both, they know each customer's history. A follow-up call can reference the quote they gave last Tuesday. A returning customer doesn't have to explain themselves again.

Dedicated agent vs. shared call center

A shared call center typically charges per minute or per call, and the agent who answers changes every time. Scripts are generic, and agents can't easily work inside your scheduling software. It suits low volume and simple message-taking.

A dedicated agent costs a flat monthly fee. The same person answers every shift, learns your price sheet and technicians, logs in to your CRM, and can book, dispatch and follow up. For businesses with steady daily calls, this usually delivers far more booked work per dollar.

How to choose a call center partner

  • Will I have the same agent every day? Consistency is what lets an agent learn your business.
  • Can I interview them first? You should hear the voice that will represent your company.
  • Will they work in my software? Booking directly into your system avoids double entry and missed jobs.
  • How is pricing calculated? Per-minute pricing rises with volume. Flat monthly pricing doesn't.
  • Is there a long-term contract? Month-to-month terms keep the provider accountable.
  • What happens when the agent is sick? A briefed backup should cover the line.

Top Dispatch answers yes to the first three, charges a flat monthly fee, runs month to month and provides backup cover.

Why our call center agents are in the Philippines

The Philippines has one of the world's largest English-speaking customer service workforces, with deep experience serving American customers. Agents work the Philippine night shift to cover your US day, in Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific time. More on why the Philippines.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an outsourced call center cost?

Top Dispatch charges a flat monthly fee based on the hours and tasks you need. There are no setup fees or per-minute charges. See pricing or ask for a quote.

Is this a shared call center?

No. You get one dedicated agent who works on your account, not a pool of agents answering many businesses.

Can the agent make outbound calls?

Yes. Follow-ups, confirmations, reminders and reactivation calls can be combined with inbound work.

What hours can the call center cover?

Your business hours in your US time zone, plus evenings, weekends or overnight if you need them.

How fast can we start?

Do you only work with home service businesses?

Yes. Your agent logs in to your CRM, phone system and scheduling software as a user.

No long-term contract. Service is month to month with 30 days' notice.
