Who We Are

Founded in 2023, Top Dispatch was built with one mission: to give service businesses access to world-class virtual secretaries at a professional price point.

Our team of highly trained professionals based in the Philippines brings 2 to 5 years of hands-on experience in appointment setting, cold calling, dispatch coordination and back-office management.

We specialize in home service industries, from HVAC and roofing to locksmith and plumbing, giving your business the professional support it deserves.

We are headquartered in Beverly Hills, California, and serve owners in every US time zone.
