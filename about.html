Our Story

About Top Dispatch

Dedicated receptionists and dispatchers for US home service companies, since 2023.

Who We Are

Top Dispatch was set up in 2023 by people who had run the front office of a home service business and knew how many jobs were lost to an unanswered phone.

Instead of a call center pool, we give each client one dedicated receptionist or dispatcher. Our team works from the Philippines, covers US business hours and averages 2 to 5 years of experience in booking, dispatch, outbound follow-up and admin for American companies.

We stay narrow on purpose: home services only. That focus is why our people know the difference between a no-heat call and a tune-up, or a burst pipe and a dripping tap.

We are headquartered in Beverly Hills, California, and serve owners in every US time zone.

How We Work With Clients

Most outsourced phone services are built for volume: a large pool of agents answers many companies' lines from a generic script and bills by the minute. That model is fine for taking messages. It struggles when a caller needs a quote, a booked slot or a technician sent to a flooded basement.

We built Top Dispatch the other way round. Each client gets one person who learns their business, works inside their own software and is measured on jobs booked, not minutes logged. If that person is out, a briefed backup covers the line so callers never notice.

What we do

  • Inbound reception, booking and dispatch
  • After-hours and weekend coverage with your emergency rules
  • Outbound follow-up, confirmations and reactivation calls
  • Back-office work such as invoicing support, inbox and CRM upkeep

What we don't do

  • Charge per minute or per call
  • Lock you into a long-term contract
  • Give clinical, legal or financial advice to your customers
  • Monitor alarm signals, which belongs with your licensed central station
