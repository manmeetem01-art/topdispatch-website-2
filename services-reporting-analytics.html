What reports should a home service business track weekly?

Most home service owners get the clearest picture from a short weekly set: calls received and answered, booking rate, jobs completed, average ticket, open estimates and their value, and cancellations. A Top Dispatch assistant compiles these from your phone system and field-service software and sends them on the schedule you choose.

Why most owners don't know their numbers, and why it matters

Field-service software is full of data, but pulling it into something useful takes time few owners have. As a result, decisions about hiring, marketing and pricing are often made on gut feel.

A short, consistent weekly report changes that. When you see calls, bookings, completed jobs, average ticket and open estimates every Monday, problems show up early: a marketing channel that stopped producing, a tech whose callbacks are rising, or estimates that are going cold.

Your Top Dispatch assistant builds that report from the tools you already use and sends it on the same day every week, so the numbers become a habit rather than a project.

Reporting traps to avoid

Too many metrics

A report nobody reads is worse than none. Start small.

Inconsistent timing

Weekly numbers only help if they arrive weekly.

Messy source data

Clean CRM records first, or the report will mislead you.

No owner for follow-up

Each flagged item needs a person and a deadline.

Reporting & Analytics FAQ

Straight answers to the questions owners ask most.

Do I need special software for reporting?

No. Reports are built from the systems you already have, using exports or built-in report tools.

Can reports go to my whole team?

Yes. We send them to whoever you choose, in email, a shared sheet or your CRM.

Is reporting a separate hire?

Usually not. Reporting is often added to a receptionist's or back-office assistant's weekly tasks.

What if my data is messy?

Cleaning up job and customer records is part of back-office support and makes reports reliable.

Can reports include marketing spend?

Yes, if you share spend figures, reports can show cost per booked job by channel.

Can I get a dashboard instead of a PDF?

Yes. We can maintain a shared spreadsheet or the dashboard in your existing tools.

How long until the first report?

Usually within the first two weeks, once access and metrics are agreed.

Is reporting available on its own?

It's usually added to a receptionist or back-office role, which keeps cost down.
