Why Cleaning Calls Need a Specialist

Cleaning companies win and keep customers on reliability and responsiveness. A prospective client who calls for a quote and gets voicemail usually tries the next company, and a recurring client who can't reach anyone to reschedule starts looking elsewhere.

Cleaning scheduling is also detailed: recurring frequencies, crew assignments, access instructions, pets, special requests and frequent reschedules.

A dedicated receptionist gives quotes from your pricing, books one-time and recurring cleans, handles changes and keeps crews informed, so the office runs without the owner being on the phone all day.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a cleaning intake script you approve. These are the questions most cleaning owners start with:

  1. Is this a one-time, recurring, move-in/out or deep clean?
  2. How many bedrooms and bathrooms, and roughly what square footage?
  3. Any pets, and are they kept away during cleaning?
  4. How will the crew get in: key, code or someone home?
  5. Any areas to skip or special requests?
  6. Preferred day and frequency?
  7. Residential or commercial?
  8. Any allergies or product preferences?
