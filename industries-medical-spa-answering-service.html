Why Medical Spa Calls Need a Specialist

Medical spas compete on experience. The first phone call sets the tone, and a rushed, unanswered or confused call can lose a client before they ever walk in.

Med spa scheduling is also detailed: consultations, treatment series, follow-ups, provider availability and pre-treatment instructions.

A dedicated receptionist handles booking, intake and follow-up with polish, and passes clinical questions to your licensed staff.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a medical spa intake script you approve. These are the questions most medical spa owners start with:

  1. Is this a first consultation or an existing client?
  2. Which treatment are you interested in?
  3. Have you had this treatment before?
  4. Preferred provider, if any?
  5. Preferred days and times?
  6. How did you hear about us?
  7. Are you interested in packages or memberships?
  8. Best contact method for reminders?

Medical Spa Answering Service FAQ

What medical spa owners ask before they sign up.

Do your receptionists give medical advice?

No. Clinical questions go to your licensed providers. Receptionists handle scheduling, intake and general information.

Which booking systems do you use?

Boulevard, Mangomint, Zenoti, Vagaro and others.

Can they run promotions?

Yes, outreach calls and messages for your current offers.

How is client privacy handled?

Receptionists follow your privacy policies and access only the systems their tasks require.

Do you handle deposits?

We direct clients to your secure payment link and never take card numbers by phone.

Can you send intake forms?

Yes, through your booking system or email.

Do you answer questions about treatments?

General information you provide is shared. Clinical questions go to licensed providers.

Can you manage membership renewals?

Yes, including reminder calls before renewal.
