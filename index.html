Our Story

Who We Are

Top Dispatch started in 2023 to fix a problem every small trade business knows: the phone rings while everyone is on a job, and the work goes to whoever answers first.

We place one dedicated, trained person with each client. Our receptionists and dispatchers work from the Philippines and bring 2 to 5 years of experience booking jobs, dispatching technicians, calling back estimates and keeping the books tidy for US companies.

We only work with home service businesses, including HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, locksmith and garage door companies, so your person learns your trade's calls instead of reading a generic script.

FAQ

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What does Top Dispatch do?

Top Dispatch places dedicated remote receptionists and dispatchers from the Philippines with US home service businesses. Your receptionist answers calls in your company's name, books jobs, dispatches technicians, follows up on estimates and keeps your CRM up to date.

Do your secretaries speak fluent English?

Yes. Every secretary passes English proficiency testing before placement and is trained to work with American customers. You interview your candidate before they start, so you can judge for yourself.

How quickly can I get started?

Most clients are live within 48 to 72 hours of signing up. We match you with a secretary who has worked in your trade, brief them on your business and they start taking calls.

What industries do you specialize in?

Home services: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, locksmith, roofing, garage door, pest control, cleaning, landscaping, construction, security, chimney, air duct and medical spa businesses.

Can I choose my own secretary?

Yes. We put forward matched candidates and you interview them before committing. If you ever want a different person, we find a replacement.

What does the service include?

Call answering, appointment setting, dispatch coordination, cold calling, back-office admin, reporting and customer support. You choose the mix, and most clients start with the phones.

What are the working hours?

Your business hours, in your time zone. If you need early starts, evenings, weekends or overnight cover, we build the schedule around that.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No long-term contract. Service runs month to month with no setup fees. You can scale up, scale down or cancel with 30 days' notice.

How do you ensure quality?

Every secretary is vetted, English-tested and trained on your industry before placement. You get call summaries and reports, and a dedicated account manager reviews performance with you.
