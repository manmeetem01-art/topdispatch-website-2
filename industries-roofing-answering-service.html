Why Roofing Calls Need a Specialist

Roofing leads come in waves. After a storm, phones can ring all day for a week, and the contractor who answers and books inspections fastest wins most of the work. Missed calls during a storm surge are very difficult to recover.

Roofing also has a long sales cycle. Inspections become estimates, estimates wait on insurance adjusters, and homeowners compare bids. Consistent follow-up through that process often decides who signs.

A dedicated receptionist qualifies every lead, books inspections for your estimators and keeps follow-up on schedule, so your sales team spends time on roofs instead of on the phone.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a roofing intake script you approve. These are the questions most roofing owners start with:

  1. Is water coming in right now, and where?
  2. When did the damage happen, and was there a storm?
  3. What's the roof material: shingle, tile, metal or flat?
  4. Roughly how old is the roof?
  5. Has an insurance claim been filed, and is an adjuster scheduled?
  6. Is it a single-family home, multi-family or commercial?
  7. Best time for an inspection, and will someone be home?
  8. How did you hear about us?

Roofing Answering Service FAQ

Yes. We plan extra coverage after major weather so no lead is lost.
