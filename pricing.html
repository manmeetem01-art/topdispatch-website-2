Pricing FAQ

How much does a virtual receptionist cost?

Top Dispatch charges a flat monthly fee based on the hours you need and the work involved, with no setup fees. Tell us what you want covered and we send a quote, usually the same day.

Are there per-minute or per-call charges?

No. You pay for a dedicated person's hours, not for each call or minute.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Service is month to month and you can scale up, scale down or cancel with 30 days' notice.

What affects the price?

Hours per day, shift timing (business hours versus overnight), and the mix of work such as reception, dispatch, outbound calling or back office.

How does this compare with hiring locally?

A remote secretary avoids payroll taxes, benefits, office space and recruiting time. Use the calculator on this page to see what missed calls are costing you today.
