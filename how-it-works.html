How It Works

Three steps from sign-up to a dedicated secretary answering your phones.

01

Sign Up & Tell Us Your Needs

Fill out a quick form about your business, industry and the type of support you need. It takes less than five minutes.

02

Get Matched With Your Secretary

We hand-pick a secretary with experience in your industry. You interview them before they start, so you feel confident.

03

Start Growing Your Business

Your secretary is briefed on your business and starts handling calls, bookings and back-office tasks. You focus on the field.

Your Onboarding Timeline

Day 0

Tell us what you need

Short intake form: trade, hours, tasks. Under five minutes.

Day 1

Meet your match

We put forward secretaries with experience in your trade. You interview and choose.

Day 2

Briefing & access

We set up logins, load your scripts and price sheet, and test call forwarding.

Day 3

First shift

Your secretary starts answering, booking and dispatching. You get a daily summary.

Week 2+

Optimise

We review results with you and add tasks such as follow-ups or back office.

What We Need From You

Gather these before your briefing and your secretary sounds like part of the team from the first call.

  • Your greeting and how you want calls answered
  • Your price sheet, fees and services you don't offer
  • Your service area by ZIP code or map
  • Technicians, skills and on-call rota
  • Logins for your phone system and job software
  • Emergency rules and escalation contacts

Onboarding FAQ

How long does onboarding take?

Most clients are live within 48 to 72 hours of signing up.

Do I interview the candidate?

Yes. You interview your matched secretary before anything starts.

How do calls reach my secretary?

Usually by forwarding your business line or adding your secretary to your phone system's ring group. Your number doesn't change.

What happens in the first week?

Your secretary shadows your scripts, you review call summaries daily and we fine-tune the rules together.
