Why Pest Control Calls Need a Specialist

Pest control revenue is built on recurring service. Every new customer who signs a quarterly or monthly plan is worth far more than a single treatment, so how calls are answered and converted has a long tail.

Pest calls also need careful identification. Ants, termites, rodents, bed bugs and wildlife involve different treatments, technicians and pricing, and some need inspection first.

A dedicated receptionist identifies the pest, explains your plans, books the initial service and keeps routes and re-services organised, so your technicians spend their day treating, not scheduling.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a pest control intake script you approve. These are the questions most pest control owners start with:

  1. What pest are you seeing, and where in the home?
  2. How long has it been happening?
  3. Any children or pets in the home?
  4. Is this a house, apartment or commercial property?
  5. Have you had treatment before?
  6. For termites: any mud tubes, damaged wood or swarmers?
  7. Are you interested in a one-time or recurring plan?
  8. Best day for service?
