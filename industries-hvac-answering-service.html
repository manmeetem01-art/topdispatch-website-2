What does an HVAC answering service do?

An HVAC answering service answers your heating and cooling company's calls, asks the right diagnostic questions (system age, brand, thermostat, filter, who is at home), books service windows, dispatches emergencies to your on-call technician and books seasonal tune-ups and maintenance-plan visits. HVAC Answering Service FAQ

What HVAC owners ask before they sign up.

Can an HVAC answering service handle emergency calls?

Yes. Your receptionist follows your rules for what counts as an emergency, such as no heat below a set temperature or a vulnerable occupant, and dispatches or pages your on-call tech.

Do your receptionists understand HVAC terms?

They are trained on HVAC call types and vocabulary, such as condensers, heat pumps, short cycling and refrigerant, so callers don't have to explain the basics.

Can they sell maintenance plans?

They can offer and renew plans using your script and pricing, and book the first visit.

How do you handle peak season?

We plan for summer and winter spikes with longer shifts or a second receptionist so calls don't go to voicemail.
