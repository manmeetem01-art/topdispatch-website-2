Why Chimney Calls Need a Specialist

Chimney companies have a short, intense season. When the first cold week arrives, everyone wants a sweep before their first fire, and calls pile up exactly when crews are fully booked.

Chimney calls also include safety issues, such as smoke entering the room or a chimney fire, and repair work that needs inspection first.

A dedicated receptionist books sweeps and inspections, triages safety concerns, schedules repairs and runs reminder calls before the season so your calendar fills early.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a chimney intake script you approve. These are the questions most chimney owners start with:

  1. Is this a sweep, an inspection or a repair?
  2. Wood, gas or pellet appliance?
  3. When was it last swept or inspected?
  4. Any smoke coming into the room or a strong odor?
  5. Any visible damage, leaks or animals in the chimney?
  6. Is this for a home sale or purchase?
  7. Number of flues?
  8. Preferred appointment window?
