What does a security answering service do?

An answering service for security companies handles non-monitoring calls: booking installations and service visits, updating customer accounts and contacts, renewing monitoring plans and dispatching technicians for faulty equipment. Top Dispatch provides a dedicated receptionist for security and alarm businesses. Alarm signal monitoring itself stays with your licensed central station.

Why Security Calls Need a Specialist

Security and alarm companies have two kinds of calls: signal monitoring, which belongs with a licensed central station, and everything else, including installs, service calls, account changes, billing and renewals. The second group is where small and mid-sized dealers often fall behind.

Those calls protect recurring monthly revenue. A customer who can't get a service visit or a quick answer on billing is a cancellation risk.

A dedicated receptionist handles the non-monitoring side: booking installs and service, updating accounts, renewing contracts and following your retention script.

Intake Questions We Ask

Your receptionist works from a security intake script you approve. These are the questions most security owners start with:

  1. Is this a new installation, a service issue or an account question?
  2. What system and panel brand?
  3. What does the keypad or app show?
  4. Residential or commercial?
  5. Is the customer the account holder?
  6. Any cameras or smart-home devices involved?
  7. Preferred appointment time?
  8. Contract or renewal date, if known?

Security Answering Service FAQ

What security owners ask before they sign up.

Do you monitor alarm signals?

No. Signal monitoring stays with your licensed central station. We handle customer service, scheduling and dispatch calls.

Can they renew contracts?

Yes, using your renewal offers and script.

Do they handle cancellations?

They follow your retention script and escalate as needed.

Can they book installs?

Yes, including site survey and equipment details.

Do you replace our central station?

No. Signal monitoring stays with your licensed central station.

Can you update emergency contacts on accounts?

Yes, following your verification process.

Do you handle camera troubleshooting?

Basic steps your script allows; complex issues are booked as service visits.

Can you call about contract renewals?

Yes, ahead of renewal dates, using your offers.
