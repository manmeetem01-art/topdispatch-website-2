Why the Philippines?

A large, experienced, English-speaking workforce that already works US hours.

Why hire a virtual receptionist from the Philippines?

The Philippines combines strong English proficiency, deep familiarity with American business culture and one of the world's largest IT and business-process workforces, much of which already works US hours. That makes it a practical place to hire experienced receptionists and dispatchers for US home service businesses at a lower total cost than a local hire.

Fluent English Speakers

Filipino professionals speak clear, neutral English, so your American customers have natural conversations every time.

US Business Culture

Deeply familiar with American business etiquette, customer expectations and home service industry standards.

Cost-Effective Solution

Get experienced professional support without the payroll taxes, benefits and overhead of a local hire.

Your Receptionist Works Your Clock

Manila runs 12 to 16 hours ahead of the mainland US, depending on daylight saving time. Your secretary works the Philippine night shift so they are fully on during your business day, whether you are in New York, Chicago, Denver or Los Angeles.

Experience that transfers

Many of our secretaries come from US-facing call centers and BPO roles where they handled scheduling, customer service and outbound calling. We add trade-specific training so they understand furnaces, sewer lines and torsion springs before day one.

Hiring in the Philippines FAQ

Is it safe to hire a virtual assistant from the Philippines?

Working through a managed provider like Top Dispatch means candidates are vetted, trained and supervised, with replacements if a fit isn't right, and access is limited to the tools you share.

Will a Filipino receptionist understand American customers?

Our secretaries are trained on US business culture, home service vocabulary and customer expectations, and you interview them before they start.

How do time zones work?

Manila is UTC+8, roughly 12 to 16 hours ahead of the mainland US. Your secretary works night shift there to cover your day here.

Is it cheaper than a local hire?

Usually, because there are no US payroll taxes, benefits, office costs or recruiting fees. You pay one flat monthly fee.
